# 服务流转

收银平台是 app_id 为 pay-core 的支付服务的别名。
收银平台调用 app_id 为 order 的订单服务。
app_id 为 pay-demo 的支付服务与 app_id 为 pay-core 的支付服务不同。

# 合同条款

## Confidentiality
Source Code must remain secret for two years.

## Confidentiality
Customer Data must remain secret for five years.

## Termination
Either party may terminate upon thirty days' written notice.
The Termination clause is also titled Early Exit; this is the same thirty-day notice clause.
