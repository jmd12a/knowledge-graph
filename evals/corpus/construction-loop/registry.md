# Settlement platform service registry

## East payment gateway

The East deployment of Payment Gateway is registered with app_id pay-east. Its region-specific operational alias is PGW-East. On-call staff also use the short label PGW in regional channels.

## West payment gateway

The West deployment of Payment Gateway is registered with app_id pay-west. Its region-specific operational alias is PGW-West. On-call staff also use the short label PGW in regional channels. The East and West deployments have independent registrations.

## Clearing services

The production Clearing Service is registered with app_id clearing-prod. It handles live settlement requests.

The Sandbox Clearing Service is registered with app_id clearing-test. It handles connectivity probes using synthetic transactions.
